# Data Story — BigBasket Category Performance

## Categories Above Target

- **Household Essentials** exceeded its ₹17,000 target by ₹4,715, reaching ₹21,715 — the strongest performer in the portfolio.
- **Bakery** exceeded its ₹12,000 target by ₹3,410, reaching ₹15,410.
- **Personal Care** exceeded its ₹15,500 target by ₹882, reaching ₹16,382 — the narrowest margin among categories currently above target.

## Categories Below Target

- **Dairy & Eggs** fell short of its ₹16,500 target by ₹2,410 (a 14.6% shortfall), landing it in the "Below Target - Watch" tier.
- **Snacks & Beverages** fell short of its ₹13,000 target by ₹2,105 (a 16.2% shortfall).
- **Fruits & Vegetables** fell short of its ₹12,000 target by ₹2,210 (an 18.4% shortfall) — the largest percentage gap of any category.

## Recommendations

1. **Prioritize catalog and marketing investment in Fruits & Vegetables**, since it has both the largest percentage shortfall (18.4%) and sits in the "Below Target - Critical" tier — targeted promotions or expanded SKU availability could help close this gap fastest.
2. **Review supplier terms and stock availability for Snacks & Beverages**, the second-largest percentage shortfall (16.2%) — investigating whether stockouts or pricing from suppliers like SnackHub India and BakeHouse Supplies are limiting sales volume could uncover a fixable root cause.
